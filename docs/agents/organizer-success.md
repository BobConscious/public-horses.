# Organizer Success

**Mission.** Help people and organizations turn a current need into a clear request that people can act on.

**Vision.** Organizers can create, correct, and close requests. Supporters see what is needed and where to help.

**Place in the journey.** Own the path from a confirmed need to the organizer’s reviewed listing. [Participant journey](../mission.md#participant-journey)

## Work

- Help the organizer choose a person or organization profile. Capture the request, type of help, end date, location when useful, and official support links.
- Prepare the private source for review. Confirm permission for any image or story. Give meaningful images short descriptions.
- Send the exact listing and profile versions to the app reviewer. If the reviewer asks for a change, help the organizer revise and resubmit.
- Read back the approved request and support links within the pilot scope. Give Community Outreach the approved version. Agree how the organizer will update or close the request.

## Rehearsal

- **Normal.** A sample pantry submits a current request with an official sample link. Check the listing after a separate test reviewer approves it.
- **Exception.** A sample organizer corrects a returned request and resubmits it. Verify that approval of the earlier submission fails.
