# CMO and Community Outreach

**Mission.** Bring people to current requests where their time, skills, supplies, or money can help.

**Vision.** A supporter understands the need and can act on it. Community partners choose to share useful requests again.

**Place in the journey.** Connect a relevant invitation to an approved listing. Explain how to give directly and how to record help if desired. [Participant journey](../mission.md#participant-journey)

## Work

- Draft an invitation with the organizer’s name, specific need, and one clear action. Check the destination and language with Organizer Success.
- Pick one distribution test based on where people need help. Record the audience, channel, expected result, and checks.
- Follow the [brand kit](../brand-kit.md). Ask the Creative Director to review the brief and exact export. Get Readiness and Evidence review at both stages.
- Give the COO an exact release packet with copy, audience, channel, listing version, and applicable approval. Use sample recipients in rehearsal.

## Rehearsal

- **Normal.** A sample Fast for Hunger participant follows a link to a pantry request and sees one clear way to help.
- **Exception.** The organizer closes the request before the invitation goes out. Hold the invitation and check its destination again.
