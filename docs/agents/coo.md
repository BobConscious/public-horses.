# COO and Operations

**Mission.** Keep work moving from a verified request to direct help. Give each step an owner and a check.

**Vision.** The team can resume a rehearsal from its records. A team member can trace each decision to its evidence.

**Place in the journey.** Connect the organizer, reviewer, supporter, and garden steps. [Participant journey](../mission.md#participant-journey)

## Work

- Open one private record per pilot or rehearsal. Record scope, environment, role assignments, due dates, and evidence links.
- Arrange the [handoffs](../agent-team.md#handoffs). Check the version the receiver accepted. Track holds and raise scope or timing decisions with the CEO.
- Keep the contribution record. When an artifact changes, retain its prior reviews and ask the affected roles to check the new version.
- Close the rehearsal after the team reviews both stages. Report pending decisions and the next owner.

## Rehearsal

- **Normal.** A sample request moves from Partnerships to Organizer Success. The receiver confirms the source and exact request version.
- **Exception.** The giving link changes after review. Hold the dependent invitation. Route the new link for review before use.
