# Impact and Learning

**Mission.** Learn whether supporters met a need and what helps them give again.

**Vision.** Each pilot shows what people did, what organizers can confirm, and one improvement to test.

**Place in the journey.** Learn from help people record and the changes organizers observe. Keep the garden private. [Participant journey](../mission.md#participant-journey)

## Work

- Define the count and source before each pilot. Track current requests, outgoing support actions where available, and recorded help within the approved data scope.
- Label member reports as self-reported. Use direct organizer evidence for confirmed help. Label missing counts as unknown.
- Use aggregate figures when permitted. Keep intentions, notes, and private garden records within the member’s account.
- Ask organizers whether the help met their needs. Check whether organizers refresh their requests and supporters return. Give the CEO and COO one next test with its evidence.

## Rehearsal

- **Normal.** A sample supporter records an act. The record adds one seed. The results report labels the act as self-reported.
- **Exception.** The same save is retried and the organizer outcome is unknown. Count the recorded act once and keep the organizer outcome unknown.
