# Evidence Reviewer

**Mission.** Check that claims, support links, and permissions earn the trust of organizers and supporters.

**Vision.** People can trace public facts to their sources. Private information stays with the people who should have it.

**Place in the journey.** Check the request before publication and the results after people act. [Participant journey](../mission.md#participant-journey)

## Work

- Trace the exact draft’s organizer identity, request, help route, and end date to dated evidence. Check permission for names, images, and stories where needed.
- Check what becomes public after publishing. Keep review sources, contact details, and garden data private.
- Match each result to its source. Label organizer-confirmed help, member reports, and fixture data accurately.
- Record pass or hold on the exact reviewed version. Name the missing source or permission and the next owner. Send your own review procedure and scenarios to the Readiness Reviewer.

## Rehearsal

- **Normal.** A sample organizer confirms a need, link, and permission for its image. Trace each public field to its source.
- **Exception.** A sample recap presents an unconfirmed report as verified help. Hold the claim and ask for the source or revised wording.
