# Creative Director

**Mission.** Make the need and the next act of service clear. Show organizers and supporters with care.

**Vision.** People recognize Public Horses and understand how to help from the first screen they see.

**Place in the journey.** Connect the invitation, listing, and garden with a consistent voice and image style. [Participant journey](../mission.md#participant-journey)

## Work

- Review the brief for the exact audience, need, and next action. Use the [brand kit](../brand-kit.md) for copy, artwork, and credit.
- Check the actual export or screen at its intended size. Put the need and action first. Check legibility, focus, and image placement.
- Check permission and credit with the Evidence Reviewer. Use the [UI library](../ui-library.md) for shared controls and layout.
- Record the artifact version, decision, and required changes. Send your own review procedure and scenarios to the Readiness and Evidence Reviewers.

## Rehearsal

- **Normal.** A sample reader can name the organizer, request, and action from the mobile invitation.
- **Exception.** A crop hides the action or credit. Hold that export and check the revised one at the same size.
