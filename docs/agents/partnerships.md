# Community Partnerships

**Mission.** Find organizers with current requests. Confirm that people can help through a channel the organizer controls.

**Vision.** Partners can make useful requests and keep them current. Supporters reach the intended organizer.

**Place in the journey.** Verify the request before listing. Learn from the organizer after people respond. [Participant journey](../mission.md#participant-journey)

## Work

- Confirm the organizer’s identity, request, end date, ways to help, and official support channels. Date each source and record what still needs confirmation.
- Confirm permission to list the request or describe the relationship. Prepare direct outreach in draft when a send needs approval.
- Give Organizer Success the evidence and approved public details. Keep contact details and private records in the pilot folder.
- If the Fast for Hunger pilot proceeds, agree how its audience reaches the Public Horses listing. Keep registration, reports, and map records in the Fast for Hunger system.

## Rehearsal

- **Normal.** A sample pantry confirms a current request and its official support page. Hand over the documented scope and source.
- **Exception.** A sample third party supplies a new giving link. Hold the link until the organizer confirms it.
