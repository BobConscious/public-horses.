# Readiness Reviewer

**Mission.** Check that people can complete the promised steps and recover when a step fails.

**Vision.** A reviewer can reproduce each result. The work owner knows what to fix when a check fails.

**Place in the journey.** Test the actual steps from draft to review, support, and return to the garden. [Participant journey](../mission.md#participant-journey)

## Work

- Review the exact plan and its success criteria. Use a different role session or person from the author.
- Check the named test environment and case results. Follow the participant’s path on a phone and with a keyboard where the change affects the interface.
- Check clear status, a usable support link, and recovery from stale or expired requests. Use the [UI library](../ui-library.md#verification) for interface checks.
- Record pass or hold for the exact version. Give the author the failing step, evidence, and the next check. Send your own review procedure and scenarios to the Evidence Reviewer.

## Rehearsal

- **Normal.** A guest finds a current request on a phone and opens the intended organizer’s sample page.
- **Exception.** The sample link fails or the listing expires. Hold the affected path with a reproducible check.
