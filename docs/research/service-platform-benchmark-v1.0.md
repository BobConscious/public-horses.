# Service platform research

**Version** 1.0 · **Date** November 22, 2026 · **Public Horses baseline** `d58f7ec`

## How we chose the five

We ranked platforms by how closely they help people post, find, fulfill, or repeat an act of service. Each choice has published evidence of use and a lesson for the pilot. We also examined POINT and Golden for shift and attendance tools. GivePulse gave us a current public listing and a food bank's own account of how it verifies service. [POINT workflow](https://pointapp.org/nonprofit-features/sign-up-and-check-in/) · [Golden attendance guide](https://support.goldenvolunteer.com/knowledge_base/how-can-i-track-hours-and-take-attendance) · [Food bank instructions](https://www.elpasoansfightinghunger.org/volunteer)

The rank measures fit for Public Horses. Each count uses its source's period and unit. The reasons below are ideas to test. The sources report use and activity.

## 1. Idealist and VolunteerMatch

**What people can do.** Search the combined board by location, date, or skill. Approved organizations can post volunteer roles. They can use Idealist applications or give a direct way to respond. The site also collects one-day opportunities. [Merger FAQ](https://www.idealist.org/en/about/merger-faq) · [Volunteer search](https://www.idealist.org/en/help/how-do-i-search-for-volunteer-opportunities-on-idealist) · [Posting guide](https://www.idealist.org/en/help/how-do-i-post-a-volunteer-opportunity) · [One-day opportunities](https://www.idealist.org/one-time-volunteer-opportunities)

**Success evidence.** The 2025 report counts 99,000 volunteer opportunity records brought over during the merger. Its recruitment section reports 661,464 connections between people and organizations. That section covers volunteers, staff, and interns. The first count measures records moved. The second measures reported connections. [2025 impact report](https://www.idealist.org/en/insights/2025-impact-report)

**Why it may work.** A person can find a task that fits their time and place. A direct response preserves the organizer's usual process.

**Public Horses lesson.** Make one short task easy to find by place and time. Give it one clear response action.

## 2. JustServe

**What people can do.** Registered people and community organizations can submit projects for free. A representative reviews each submission. The form asks where and when the service will occur. [Posting guide](https://www.justserve.org/content/post-a-project?lang=eng)

**Success evidence.** Its sponsor reported 114,639 new users and 38,597 new projects in 2025. JustServe passed one million registered users in that year. These counts measure registrations and project additions. [2025 Caring Report](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/caring-report-2025-church-jesus-christ-latter-day-saints)

**Why it may work.** Local representatives help organizers prepare usable requests. A trusted local group can give people a reason to look for those requests. [About JustServe](https://www.justserve.org/content/about?lang=eng) · [Service guide](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/justserve-community-service-guidebook/frequently-asked-questions?lang=eng)

**Public Horses lesson.** Start with one trusted organizer. Give a named reviewer the exact request and the organizer's response link.

## 3. GivePulse

**What people can do.** A public event can show the role, shift time, number of places, and registration action. GivePulse lets an organizer or designated reviewer confirm or dispute reported service. El Pasoans Fighting Hunger asks volunteers to select a shift through GivePulse. The food bank verifies hours for people who attend and serve. [Public event](https://www.givepulse.com/event/908500-Volunteer-for-Give-Thanks-8K) · [Verification guide](https://support.givepulse.com/hc/en-us/articles/206867777-Impact-Verification) · [Food bank instructions](https://www.elpasoansfightinghunger.org/volunteer)

**Success evidence.** GivePulse reports more than 650,000 groups and organizations on its site. The page gives a count of groups and organizations without a period. [GivePulse site](https://learn.givepulse.com/)

**Why it may work.** A volunteer can see what is available. The organizer has a way to confirm what took place.

**Public Horses lesson.** Let the organizer keep availability current on the response page. Record when the organizer confirms the result.

## 4. Catchafire

**What people can do.** Nonprofit teams can choose a project outline with defined work, skills, and results. Volunteers can take on a project or offer a one-hour call. [Posting guide](https://help.catchafire.org/en/articles/1971552-for-organizations-a-guide-to-posting-projects) · [Ways to volunteer](https://help.catchafire.org/en/articles/8380963-what-is-catchafire-and-how-do-i-join)

**Success evidence.** Its February 2026 review shows 93,363 matches and 16,369 organizations matched over its history. A September 2026 report counts more than 250 completed projects in one partnership in California and Washington. That partnership started in 2023. The figures cover different groups and periods. [2025 review](https://blog.catchafire.org/no-one-shows-up-like-catchafire-our-2025-year-in-review) · [Match counts](https://blog.catchafire.org/hs-fs/hubfs/Group%2026086497%20%282%29.png?height=390&name=Group+26086497+%282%29.png&width=800) · [Partnership results](https://blog.catchafire.org/1m-in-impact-magic-cabinets-investment-in-nonprofit-capacity-building)

**Why it may work.** A project outline helps an organizer write a clear request. A volunteer can estimate the work before offering help.

**Public Horses lesson.** Give the organizer one short example. State the task, time needed, materials, and what counts as complete.

## 5. GoodGym

**What people can do.** Local hosts offer weekly sessions for practical tasks. A session report can link to the next session. The Norwich Foodbank listing shows a task, a time, a meeting place, and a host. [Weekly sessions](https://www.goodgym.org/group-runs) · [Norwich Foodbank listing](https://www.goodgym.org/happenings/sorting-donations-norwich-foodbank-9dbea34a-85e1-49d6-80d0-3aaade841e5d) · [Report with next session](https://www.goodgym.org/v3/reports/goods-to-go)

**Success evidence.** GoodGym reports about 36,000 acts of service by 3,135 runners in 2025. This count shows recorded activity across the year. [2025 review](https://www.goodgym.org/2025)

**Why it may work.** One known place and a next date make it easier to serve again.

**Public Horses lesson.** Offer one current next task after service. Give people a private place to consider what they want to cultivate through service.

## Apply the findings

Use one clear request, one direct response link, and one organizer-confirmed result. Test a next task before adding new tools. [Pilot plan v1.0](../plans/service-board-learning-v1.0.md)
