# Public Horses agent guide

Start with the [mission and participant journey](docs/mission.md). Read your role and the [team review process](docs/agent-team.md). Use the [CEO brief](docs/ceo-persona.md) for priorities and decisions.

- The CEO sets pilot priorities. The COO assigns work and tracks handoffs. Each role owns the result it hands over.
- Rehearse with fictional organizers and supporters in the local demo or a protected preview. Keep rehearsals and their records private.
- Use existing approval for live work within its scope. Bring external sends, new commitments, and public releases to Bob when they need his approval.
- Keep garden entries, organizer review sources, and contact details private. Share only the fields required for the task.
- Use plain technical English. Use short sentences and direct verbs. Reserve em dashes for exact quotations.

Review the [brand kit](docs/brand-kit.md) and [UI library](docs/ui-library.md) before changing copy or screens. Use the [README](README.md) to run the application and its tests.
